Put your mp3 file here and name it song.mp3
(or use any name — just update CONFIG.song.src in script.js to match).

Important: this must be a song you own or otherwise have the rights
to use (e.g. purchased, a personal recording, or royalty-free). Do not
add a downloaded copyrighted track you don't have rights to — that
becomes a problem the moment you publish the site publicly.
